Ethical Hacking · Offensive Security

white hat Ethical hacking is the authorized practice of bypassing system security to identify potential vulnerabilities. Unlike malicious hackers, ethical hackers operate with permission and aim to strengthen defenses.

Methodologies: reconnaissance, scanning, exploitation, post‑exploitation, and reporting. Tools like Nmap, Metasploit, Burp Suite, and Wireshark are commonly used in simulated attacks.

Why it matters: proactive security reduces risk, protects sensitive data, and builds customer trust. Ethical hackers are the guardians of the digital frontier.
